# Worksheet: Shopify Subscription Stores Using Recharge

Illustrative example. Working inputs for the checker; not sent to the client.

## Campaign Inputs

| Field | Value |
|-|-|
| Mode | Audit of Lena's current touch 1 (2 replies from 600 sends); the sequence is rebuilt around the rewrite |
| Offer sentence | We help Shopify subscription brands in food and beverage keep subscribers past the second charge with a skip-a-month email sent before it. |
| Segment and buyer | Heads of ecommerce or retention at US Shopify food and beverage stores on Recharge; founders who run the store themselves get variant 1 |
| Segment markers | Shopify, Recharge |
| Their words | subscribers, second charge, skip, swap, cancel flow, renewal, box |
| Facts from the brief | Coffee subscription client on Recharge, April to May 2026 (client's Recharge cancellation report): skip was already offered in the Recharge customer portal; one skip-a-month email, sent from Klaviyo three days before the second charge, cut month two cancellations from 31% to 19% in 45 days. That client's top cancel reason was "too much coffee". Tea subscription store, June 2026: kept 41 more subscribers in the first month after adding the same email. |
| Reply offer | The coffee client's skip email, shared with the client's permission, ready to adapt; and a free written list of gaps in the emails a store sends between the first box and the second charge, kept either way. Lena confirmed she will deliver both within 3 business days of a yes. |
| Likely objection | "Subscribers can already skip in the Recharge portal." The coffee client had portal skip too, so the answer comes from the brief. |
| Timing | Mid-October 2026, before holiday gift orders start |
| List source | Lena's store export, 1,500 rows: platform Shopify, apps include Recharge, country US, category food and beverage, 10,000 to 150,000 estimated monthly visits, contact title filled |
