# Cold Email Campaign Plan: Review Software for Texas Dental Practices

Illustrative example. BrightChair Reviews (brightchair.example) is a fictional review-request SaaS; every name, client and figure below is invented to show the format.

Prepared 2026-10-13 for Dana Ortiz, Head of Growth. Sending platform: Instantly. Launch: 2026-10-26. Not checked: inbox placement of the 3 warmed inboxes, until the 2026-10-23 seed test.

## Offer

Offer sentence: We help 3 to 6 chair dental practices in Texas get from under 60 Google reviews to over 150 in 5 months by texting a review link to every patient within an hour of their visit.

| Check | Value |
|-|-|
| Proof source (client, date, metric) | Round Rock practice, May to September 2026, Google review count 41 to 163; Katy practice, 3.8 to 4.4 stars in 4 months |
| Current alternative it beats | The front desk asks happy patients in person; Round Rock added about 2 reviews a month that way |
| Objection answered in the offer | "No time to set it up": BrightChair connects the practice software in one 20 minute call |
| Ask | Resource ask ("Want the text we send?"), tested against a call ask in cell A |
| Price in replies | Price depends on locations, so it is given on the call |
| Risk reducer | 30 day pilot on one location, cancel any time |

All-reply rates: Instantly's 2026 benchmark report (3.43% average) and Hunter's State of Email Outreach 2026 (4.5% average sequence reply rate). Break-even = 100 x $290 / (delivered a month x 0.4 x 0.7 x 0.2 x $1,200 CAC ceiling): 0.69 on 624 delivered, 0.35 on 1,247.

Shortfall fix: the second domain (3 seats and a domain, $22 a month) closes the target gap from December. At floor rates no inbox count closes it within the list's runway; the goal needs 1.0 positive reply per 100 delivered at target booking rates. If cell A reads between 0.35 and 1.0 on 2026-11-19, Dana rewrites the offer from the tagged reply reasons before cell B launches; under 0.35, cell A stops. Dana holds 8 first-call slots a week, against about 2.2 bookings a week at target.
