## Where sources differ

| Topic | Sources | Rule here |
|-|-|-|
| Calling window | Federal 8am to 9pm; Texas 9am start; Florida 8pm stop | 9am to 8pm everywhere |
| Calls per day | Florida 3 per 24 hours on one topic | 1 call per day per person; 3 per 14 day cadence |
| Best call time | Berman: late afternoon local; midday weakest | Two windows: 8 to 10am and 3:30 to 5:30pm local |
| B2B and DNC | FTC: most B2B sales calls exempt; FCC applies DNC to wireless numbers that may be personal | Scrub every mobile; ask counsel before relying on the exemption |
