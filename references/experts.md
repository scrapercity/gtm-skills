# Experts and Authorities

The outbound, list-building and deliverability authorities this repo cites, who each one is, where their own work lives, what to use them for, and which skills cite them. Profiles say only what each person's or organization's own pages say, checked on 2026-10-13. Figures a person or vendor reports about themselves are marked self-reported. The "Cited in" lines come from a search of every skill folder in this repo on 2026-10-13; shared files are listed separately.

## How to cite an authority

| Rule | Detail |
|-|-|
| Attribute only what the source says | Paraphrase their own page, post, book or talk, and link it. If you cannot find the claim in their own work, cut it. |
| Quote sparingly | Under 15 words, at most one quote per source. |
| Label self-reported numbers | Meetings booked, emails sent, connect-rate gains and client results on a person's or vendor's own site are self-reported. |
| Benchmarks are hypotheses | Give the source, the sample and the date, and test against your own data before using a number as a target. |
| Vendors are interested parties | Research from a company that sells the tool it measures (Gong, Cognism, Belkins, Hunter, Instantly) is labeled vendor research. |
| Alex Berman's stat | His companies have booked 500,000+ meetings. They send millions of cold emails a month; the 500,000 figure always counts meetings. |

## Research and benchmarks (vendor research, label it as such)

### Gong Labs

- **Who:** Gong's research section, which publishes findings from calls and deals recorded in Gong. Its cold call opener study of 90,380 first-interaction calls reported "How have you been?" booking meetings at 10.01% against a 1.5% baseline.
- **Primary links:** https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/, https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/cold-call-opening-lines/
- **Use for:** opener and talk-track hypotheses to test on your own calls.
- **Cited in:** `cold-calling`

### Cognism

- **Who:** A sales intelligence vendor focused on B2B data and phone-verified mobiles, with a strong Europe focus. Its 2025 cold calling report uses calling data from WHAM ("We Have A Meeting") and recommends 3 calls per prospect, which it says captures 93% of conversations.
- **Primary links:** https://www.cognism.com/, https://www.cognism.com/cold-calling-report-2025
- **Use for:** attempts per prospect and call timing hypotheses.
- **Cited in:** `cold-calling`, `outbound-unit-economics`

### Belkins

- **Who:** A B2B lead generation company (appointment setting, cold email, cold calling, deliverability consulting). Its 2026 cold calling statistics draw on 175,000+ dials logged in 2025 and report a 9.9% per-dial connect rate and roughly 370 dials per booked meeting.
- **Primary links:** https://belkins.io/blog/cold-calling-benchmarks
- **Use for:** dial, connect and meeting-rate hypotheses for call capacity plans.
- **Cited in:** `cold-calling`, `outbound-unit-economics`

### Hunter

- **Who:** An email outreach platform with Domain Search, Email Finder, an email verifier and Sequences. Its State of Cold Email 2025 combines 11 million cold emails sent in 2024 with a survey of 217 decision-makers and reports a 4.1% average reply rate.
- **Primary links:** https://hunter.io/about, https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email-2025
- **Use for:** reply-rate baselines by campaign size and follow-up use.
- **Cited in:** `cold-email-copywriting`, `cold-email-strategy`, `multi-channel-outreach`, `outbound-unit-economics`, `reply-handling`; shared: `references/cold-email-frameworks.md`

### Instantly

- **Who:** A cold email sending platform that publishes benchmark reports. Its 2026 benchmark article puts the average reply rate at 3.43%, the top quartile at 5.5% and elite campaigns above 10%; the article gives no sample size.
- **Primary links:** https://instantly.ai/blog/email-sequence-benchmarks-2026-whats-a-good-open-rate-reply-rate-and-cost-per-meeting/
- **Use for:** reply rate and cost-per-meeting starting hypotheses.
- **Cited in:** `cold-email-strategy`, `outbound-unit-economics`; shared: `references/cold-email-frameworks.md`

## Deliverability standards and mailbox providers

- **Primary links:** https://www.m3aawg.org/, https://www.m3aawg.org/sites/default/files/doc_files/m3aawg-sender-best-common-practices-aug-27-2026.pdf
- **Use for:** sender practices behind ramp-up, list hygiene and complaint handling.
- **Cited in:** `cold-email-list`, `email-deliverability`, `email-list-cleaner`, `inbox-setup`; shared: `references/deliverability-primer.md`, `references/pitfalls-index.md`

### Laura Atkins and Steve Atkins

- **Who:** The consultants behind Word to the Wise, an email consultancy that works with senders on strategy, deliverability and infrastructure, and publishes a blog on filtering and reputation.
- **Primary links:** https://wordtothewise.com/
- **Use for:** reading a deliverability drop from the receiver's side before changing copy or volume.
- **Cited in:** no skill yet

### Al Iverson

- **Who:** Publisher of Spam Resource, which says he has written about email since 2001. The site covers DNS records, authentication, blocklists and mailbox provider changes.
- **Primary links:** https://www.spamresource.com/
- **Use for:** plain explainers on SPF, DKIM, DMARC and blocklist entries when a record or a listing needs fixing.
- **Cited in:** no skill yet

### Google (Gmail)

- **Who:** Gmail's email sender guidelines. Senders of more than 5,000 messages a day to personal Gmail accounts need SPF, DKIM and DMARC (policy `none` is accepted), a From domain aligned with SPF or DKIM, and one-click unsubscribe on marketing mail; the spam rate in Postmaster Tools must stay below 0.30%, with 0.10% as the target. Google announced the bulk sender rules for February 2024.
- **Primary links:** https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126, https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gmail/gmail-security-authentication-spam-protection/
- **Use for:** authentication, unsubscribe and complaint thresholds for mail to Gmail.
- **Cited in:** `cold-email-copywriting`, `cold-email-list`, `cold-email-strategy`, `domain-infrastructure`, `ecommerce-by-techstack`, `email-deliverability`, `email-list-cleaner`, `icp-list-builder`, `inbox-setup`, `local-business-list`, `reply-handling`, `sending-platforms`; shared: `references/cold-email-frameworks.md`, `references/deliverability-primer.md`, `references/list-quality-standard.md`

### Yahoo

- **Who:** Yahoo Sender Hub best practices. Bulk senders need SPF and DKIM, DMARC at least `p=none` with From alignment (relaxed is fine), one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058 POST recommended) honored within 2 days, and a spam rate below 0.3%.
- **Primary links:** https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/
- **Use for:** the same thresholds for mail to Yahoo and AOL addresses.
- **Cited in:** `cold-email-copywriting`, `cold-email-list`, `cold-email-strategy`, `domain-infrastructure`, `ecommerce-by-techstack`, `email-deliverability`, `email-list-cleaner`, `icp-list-builder`, `inbox-setup`, `local-business-list`, `reply-handling`, `sending-platforms`; shared: `references/cold-email-frameworks.md`, `references/deliverability-primer.md`, `references/list-quality-standard.md`

### Microsoft (Outlook consumer domains)

- **Who:** Microsoft's requirements for senders of 5,000 or more messages to its consumer email services from one From domain: SPF and DKIM pass, a published DMARC record, and DMARC pass through SPF or DKIM alignment. Mail that fails is rejected with `550 5.7.515`. The requirements were announced in April 2025 for enforcement in May 2025.
- **Primary links:** https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/fix-ndr-error-550-5-7-515-in-outlook-com, https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftdefenderforoffice365blog/strengthening-email-ecosystem-outlook%E2%80%99s-new-requirements-for-high%E2%80%90volume-senders/4399730
- **Use for:** authentication rules for mail to Outlook.com, Hotmail and Live addresses.
- **Cited in:** `cold-email-list`, `cold-email-strategy`, `domain-infrastructure`, `ecommerce-by-techstack`, `email-deliverability`, `email-list-cleaner`, `icp-list-builder`, `local-business-list`; shared: `references/deliverability-primer.md`, `references/list-quality-standard.md`

### Spamhaus

- **Who:** Publisher of the Domain Blocklist (DBL), a list of domain names with poor reputation used by mail administrators to check sending domains and links. It lists domain names only.
- **Primary links:** https://www.spamhaus.org/blocklists/domain-blocklist/, https://check.spamhaus.org/
- **Use for:** checking sending and tracking domains before and during a campaign.
- **Cited in:** `domain-infrastructure`, `email-deliverability`, `inbox-setup`; shared: `references/deliverability-primer.md`, `references/pitfalls-index.md`
